Automate taps, swipes and zooms on your phone, with your own timing between actions. No root needed.

## Features

- Multiple clicks, swipes and zooms in one sequence
- Set the delay between actions and the swipe duration
- Loop forever, for a set time, or a set number of cycles
- Floating control bar with adjustable size

## Permissions

- **Display over other apps** — shows the floating control bar and targets
- **Accessibility service** — performs the clicks and swipes on screen. It is not used to collect any of your data.

| Platform | Minimum     |
|----------|-------------|
| Android  | 7.0 (API 24) |
