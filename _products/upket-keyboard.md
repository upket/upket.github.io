Vietnamese keyboard that runs entirely on your phone. Smart suggestions, your own themes — and no Internet permission.

## Features

- Telex and VNI: type accents in any order, English words restored automatically when you mistype accents
- Word suggestions in Vietnamese and English that learn as you type, swipe typing that adds accents for you
- Personal dictionary and text shortcuts (`ko` → không, `mn` → mọi người)
- Quick results on the suggestion bar: `50000*3=`, `5km=`, `1tr5` → 1,500,000
- Clipboard that picks out OTP codes, account numbers and phone numbers; history is off by default
- 10 themes, or your own colors and a photo as the background; one-handed, floating and split layouts
- Emoji search in Vietnamese, kaomoji, voice input in Vietnamese or English
- Backup and restore to a .zip file you keep

## Privacy

- **No Internet permission** — what you type cannot leave your phone through the app
- Incognito mode: no word learning, no clipboard history
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking
