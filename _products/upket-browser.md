A simple, fast web browser for Android TV, phones and tablets — with a built-in ad blocker and the same features on every screen.

## Made for the big screen

- Smooth on-screen cursor driven by your TV remote's D-pad — no touchscreen or mouse needed
- Remote media keys control videos and music on the page
- Control the TV from your phone: scan a QR code for a touchpad, D-pad, keyboard and tab list

## Great for video

- Full-screen controls, seek bar, speed 0.5x–2x
- On phones and tablets: double-tap to skip 10 s, swipe for brightness and volume
- Picture-in-picture and background playback

## Ad and pop-up blocking

- Blocks ads and trackers on websites, on by default — EasyList, EasyPrivacy, a Vietnamese list and uBlock Origin filters
- Stops pop-ups and redirects; allow ads or pop-ups for a site with one tap

## Private and clean

- Private tabs, clear browsing data any time or on exit
- Secure DNS, HTTPS-only mode, Global Privacy Control
- No account, no ads, no analytics — history and bookmarks stay on your device

## Requirements

Android System WebView 101 or newer (the app asks you to update an older one).
