Most of my side projects used to die at 70%. They grew a settings page, a sync engine, an account system — and then I lost interest.

## The rule

Now every Upket app starts with one sentence: *what does it do in under ten words?* If I can't write that sentence, I don't start.

- One core action, reachable in one tap
- Works offline first, syncs later
- Ship a demo before polishing

> Small apps get finished. Finished apps get used.
