      <h2>Who's behind it</h2>
      <p>Hi, I'm <strong>Hien Nguyen</strong> — a developer based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. I build Upket apps end to end: design, code, release and support. I've spent the last few years building Android and web apps, and started Upket to ship the small tools I kept wishing existed.</p>
      <h2>How I build</h2>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>One job per app.</strong> Each app does one thing and tries to do it well.</li>
        <li><strong>Privacy by default.</strong> Data stays on your device unless a feature clearly needs a server — and each app's policy says exactly what is sent.</li>
        <li><strong>Ship, listen, improve.</strong> Releases are small and frequent; feedback goes straight to the person writing the code.</li>
      </ul>
